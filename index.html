Oscar

Oscar is a private household assistant used by one family only. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign up or use it.

Privacy

Oscar accesses only the one household Google account that its owner connected, and only to read and send that account's e-mail and manage its calendar on the owner's instructions. Data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
